# Reference Analysis Notes

The requested source/reference video was not present in `/home/user` when development began. The implementation therefore uses the detailed written observations supplied in the brief rather than claiming an exact video match.

## Applied with high confidence from the written reference

- Warm white/ivory page background.
- Cream jewellery workspace with a thin pale-pink border.
- Large curved gold necklace occupying most of the editor.
- Instruction: “Drag and place your charms”.
- Undo, Redo, Clear, Zoom and pink Checkout controls.
- Compact product catalogue directly below the workspace.
- Small product illustrations, uppercase names, PKR prices and selected checkmarks.
- Burgundy navigation/footer treatment.
- Floating green WhatsApp control.
- Persistent mobile bottom navigation.
- Dense two-column mobile charm catalogue.

## Intentionally configurable / unverified

- Brand identity: **SCARLET** placeholder.
- Logo/wordmark.
- Exact font family and colour values.
- Product names not legible in the written description.
- Product composition, dimensions, finish and stock.
- Store contact details, WhatsApp number and physical address.
- Delivery, returns, tax and warranty policies.
- Payment provider.

## Original demo assets

The repository includes 28 original SVG jewellery illustrations generated by `scripts/gen-charms.mjs`; they are not scraped product photography or representations of verified inventory. Each asset has a soft-background catalogue variant and a transparent necklace-canvas variant.
